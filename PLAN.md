# My City: improvement plan

> **Note:** AI made the plan, not fully read by owner. Completed and playtested items (1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11, 14–30) have been removed; full history is in `git log -p PLAN.md`.

## Goals
3. **Checkpoint runs.** A marker a few blocks away, a timer, and a saved best time (`PlayerPrefs`).
5. **Collectibles.** Coins or stars on the roads, placed from each tile's seed so they stay in the same spots.

## Living city
8. **Day and night cycle.** Lit windows (glow map on the facades) and streetlights.
9. **Variety.** Ramps, bridges, a highway or a river every few tiles.

## Polish
12. **Car choice or paint colour.** (The pause and settings menu part is built, see below.)
13. **Tilt steering** as an option on phones.

## Built, not yet marked tested
31. **Low-speed full stop.** Coasting below about 7 km/h now brakes the car to a real stop instead of creeping at 1 km/h.
32. **Start screen and pause menu.** The game opens on a title screen (city drives behind it, camera circles the taxi) with PLAY/CONTINUE, SETTINGS and QUIT. In the game, Esc, P, gamepad Start, Android back or the pause button (left of UPGRADES) open the pause menu: resume, upgrades, show/hide stats, flip car, settings, main menu, quit (PC). Settings: render quality, frame rate cap, invert camera Y, touch button size (phones), reset progress (with a confirm).

## Suggested order
Checkpoint runs (3), then car choice or paint colour (12).
